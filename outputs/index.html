Update Your LulzBot Firmware

Firmware controls your 3D printer's mechanical functions. Update it to gain new features and boost performance. Choose a local firmware image, connect to a supported LulzBot printer over USB, and write it directly from your browser.

⚠
Confirm your printer model, tool head, and options before continuing. This operation erases existing firmware. Use a firmware image for the exact printer and configuration. This page supports .bin firmware files for the LulzBot TAZ Pro and TAZ 8, and .hex firmware files for the LulzBot Mini 1, Mini 2, SideKick, Workhorse, and Workhorse 2. Warning: Updating may reset certain machine settings. Please note down any settings you might want later, such as Z-offset, backlash compensation, and nozzle offsets for dual extruders.

Before You Flash

Use a known-good firmware image for the exact board and configuration. Keep the printer powered and connected directly by USB. Do not disconnect during erase, write, or verify.

This prototype checks the controller chip, but it does not determine whether the firmware matches your printer model, toolhead, or options.

After You Flash

Re-enter the calibration values you noted before flashing, through the printer's settings or configuration menu, or recalibrate the printer using its calibration menu.

See the OHAI for calibration instructions.

If your firmware offers tool head selection, choose the tool head installed on your printer from the printer’s tool head menu.
