Confirm your board and firmware before continuingThis operation erases existing firmware. This prototype only supports SAM-BA/BOSSA compatible boards and raw .bin images. Most classic LulzBot Mini and TAZ printers use AVR/RAMBo boards and require a different flasher. The browser flash sequence has not yet been tested on a physical TAZ Pro.

TARGET BOARD

TAZ Pro · Archim2

ATSAM3X8E with BOSSA/SAM-BA bootloader. Expected flash: 512 KB at 0x80000, 2,048 pages × 256 bytes. Flash is enabled only after the ROM reports the SAM3X8E chip ID.

Still unsupported: AVR/RAMBo firmware, Intel HEX files, UF2, and other controller families.

Before you flash

Use a known-good firmware image for the exact board and configuration. Keep the printer powered and connected directly by USB. Do not disconnect during erase, write, or verify. The image is read locally and is not uploaded to the web server.

This prototype checks for an ATSAM3X8E board, but it cannot determine whether the firmware matches your printer model, toolhead, or options.

Local only · No firmware upload · Web Serial support required
